# AudioScope — Ambient Audio Intelligence Research Lab

*Provisional name. Independent research initiative, started 2026-11-17.*

## Thesis

The next generation of computing may involve machines that perceive and reason about the physical
world through sound, vision, motion and other sensory signals. Voice AI is one component of that
system, not the whole of it. We investigate the underlying science and engineering, reproduce
published work on accessible hardware, find its limits, and look for places where research has not
yet been translated into usable open implementations.

## Status (2026-11-17)

Day one. Workspace created; initial leads verified against primary sources; domain surveys in
progress. See [research/22_RESEARCH_LOG.md](research/22_RESEARCH_LOG.md) for the running log and
[research/21_DECISION_LOG.md](research/21_DECISION_LOG.md) for corrections to earlier assumptions.

## Hardware position

Windows laptop (primary compute) + smartphone (first-class sensor node) + local Wi-Fi. No
purchases until a specific experiment needs one. Hardware-independent architecture.
See [research/15_TECHNICAL_ARCHITECTURE.md](research/15_TECHNICAL_ARCHITECTURE.md).

## Research domains

A General auditory intelligence · B Spatial audio intelligence (high priority) · C Source
separation and selective hearing · D Real-time speech and full-duplex interaction · E Multilingual
communication and voice identity · F Earable computing and physiological sensing · G Silent speech
and neural interfaces · H First-person multimodal intelligence · I Industrial acoustic intelligence
· J Bioacoustics and animal communication.
